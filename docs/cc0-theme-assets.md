# Public-domain theme samples

The PNGs listed below are adapted from texture assets published by
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) under
[CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Poly Haven's logos, website and
example renders are not included. Each link points to the original asset;
authors are credited here even though CC0 does not require attribution.

Each sample was made from the original 1K JPG diffuse colour map, resized to
fit within 256 x 256 pixels while preserving its aspect ratio, and stripped of
metadata. Pattern samples were also
converted to greyscale and auto-leveled. The two Fabric Pattern samples used
the original `col_01` and `col_03` maps respectively, rather than a diffuse
map. The original download size and MD5 were checked against Poly Haven's API.

Paths are relative to `share/swordfish/` when installed, or to the source root.
